Controls

Every action button reads a swipe made while you hold it: tap for the basic action, or swipe up, down, left or right before you let go for a different pass, shot or skill. Hold a button for a moment in a match to see its swipes.

Ratings drive everything: PAC sets speed, SHO shot power and accuracy, PAS pass accuracy, DRI close control, first touch and skill-move success, DEF tackling and interceptions, PHY strength and stamina. Keepers use DIV, HAN, KIC, REF, SPD and POS. Rearrange the buttons from the pause menu (Edit button layout).
